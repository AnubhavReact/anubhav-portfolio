The summary

AI-native mobile engineer with 3.5+ years building and shipping production apps for iOS & Android — 8+ built, 6+ live on the stores, one serving 100K+ active users. I put LLMs inside products (AWS Bedrock, Gemini assistants, AI budget planning) and use AI tooling — Claude, Cursor — to ship them faster.
